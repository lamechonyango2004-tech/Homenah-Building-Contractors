Architectural & structural design

Every Homenah project is grounded in proper design work before a single block is laid — from spatial planning to load calculations.

Architectural design

We translate a client's brief into buildable drawings — floor plans, elevations and sections — checked against local planning requirements before submission.

  • Space planning and layout options
  • Elevations and exterior massing studies
  • Working drawings for approval and construction
  • Planning permission documentation
Ground floor plan — sample residential layout

Structural engineering

Load paths, foundations and framing are calculated before construction begins, so the building performs the way the drawings say it will.

  • Load calculations for roofs, floors and walls
  • Foundation design for varying soil conditions
  • Reinforced concrete, structural steel and timber framing
  • Retrofit and reinforcement of existing structures
Roof truss configuration — standard span diagram

MEP & services integration

Mechanical, electrical and plumbing runs are planned alongside the structure so services don't fight the building later.

  • Electrical layout and load distribution
  • Plumbing and drainage routing
  • Ventilation and drainage coordination with structural elements

Technical capabilities

A summary of the materials, spans and standards we design and build to. Figures are indicative — every project gets its own calculations.

Foundation typesStrip footing, raft, and pile foundations, selected by soil investigation
Structural materialsReinforced concrete, structural steel framing, engineered timber
Typical roof spanUp to 9 m clear span with standard timber trussing; greater spans with steel
Wall systemsLoad-bearing masonry, block-and-frame, and lightweight partition systems
Inspection scopeFoundations, structural frame, roofing, drainage, and finishes
ComplianceLocal county building codes and National Construction Authority requirements

How a project runs

The same sequence, every time — so you always know what stage you're at.

Consultation

We walk the site or review the brief and agree scope, budget range and timeline.

Concept design

Initial layouts and elevations for your review before anything is finalised.

Structural analysis & permitting

Engineering calculations are completed and drawings submitted for approval.

Construction

Site work proceeds in stages, each one signed off before the next begins.

Inspection & quality control

Independent checks against the approved drawings and building code.

Handover & warranty

Final walkthrough, documentation, and workmanship warranty period begins.
