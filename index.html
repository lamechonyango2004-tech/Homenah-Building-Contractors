FAQ

Helpful answers before you get started

How long does it take to build a website?

Most small business websites take a few days to a couple of weeks depending on the scope, the amount of content and how quickly feedback is provided.

Can you build a website for my small business?

Yes. I build websites for small businesses, service providers and local brands that need a clearer online presence and easier customer contact.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Mobile-first design is a core part of how I build websites so they work smoothly on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?

Yes. I can add direct WhatsApp links and chat flows so visitors can contact your business instantly.

Can you integrate M-Pesa?

Yes, when it is relevant to your business operations and the required payment workflow.

Do you provide hosting and deployment?

Yes. I can help with deployment and make sure your site is ready to go live without unnecessary complexity.

Can you help my business appear on Google?

Yes. I can support local visibility with Google Business Profile guidance and basic SEO setup as part of a practical online presence strategy.

Can I update the website later?

Yes. I build websites in a way that makes future updates and content changes straightforward as your business evolves.
